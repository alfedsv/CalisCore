1. INTRODUCTION
This Privacy Policy describes how CalisCore (the “App”) handles user information.

The App is designed with respect for your privacy. I do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

2. INFORMATION I DO NOT COLLECT
I do not collect the following categories of data:

Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, address)

Location data

Contacts

Photos or media files

Health or fitness data transmitted to servers

Device identifiers for tracking

App usage data (analytics, telemetry)

3. LOCAL DATA STORAGE
All data you enter into the App (e.g., workout settings, exercise history, preferences) is stored exclusively locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to servers, cloud services, or third parties.

When you delete the App, all local data is permanently deleted.

4. NETWORK DATA TRANSMISSION
The App does not transmit any data over the network. The App works entirely offline. The only network activity is related to downloading updates through the App Store, which is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

5. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
The App does not use third-party services that collect data:

Analytics services (Google Analytics, Firebase, etc.)

Advertising networks and SDKs

Tracking services

Social plugins

6. APPLE HEALTHKIT INTEGRATION
If the App integrates with Apple HealthKit, workout data may be written to HealthKit only with your explicit permission. This data remains on your device and is governed by Apple’s privacy policy. The App does not read data from HealthKit and does not share it with third parties.

7. CHILDREN’S PRIVACY
The App does not collect personal data from children under 13. Since the App collects no personal data at all, this requirement is automatically satisfied.

8. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
I reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy. You will be notified of changes by posting the revised version in the App or on this page. The last updated date is indicated at the beginning of this document.
